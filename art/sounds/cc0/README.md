# CC0 recordings (the Arm Cannon's and the arm blades' sounds)

Real recordings the Arm Cannon's gunfire (`tools/storm_guns.py`), its draw and reload (`tools/storm_foley.py`) and
the arm blades' sounds (`tools/blade_sounds.py`) are built from, all dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use, change and ship, no credit required (credited here
anyway).
