## Description (BBCode)

```
[center][size=6][b]Deus Ex: Revision Co-op (RevNet)[/b][/size]
[size=4]The whole Revision campaign, in online co-op.[/size]

[b][color=#ffaa33]IN DEVELOPMENT: NOT YET RELEASED.[/color][/b] This page is here to follow along; files come with the first release.[/center]

[size=5][b]What it is[/b][/size]
RevNet is online co-op for Deus Ex: Revision. You and your friends play the single-player campaign together, in one shared world, from Liberty Island to the end. Friends join through a small launcher over Steam: no port forwarding.

[list]
[*][b]Single-player rules, together.[/b] Deus Ex switches to deathmatch rules the moment it is networked; RevNet keeps the single-player game feature by feature: conversations, missions, the story, the AI.
[*][b]Co-op saves.[/b] Checkpoints with map memory: flags, goals, notes, every player's inventory and the state of every map you have visited.
[*][b]Every Revision mode.[/b] The host picks Revision, Vanilla, Shifter, Biomod or Human Revolution, the difficulty, friendly fire and the death rules; joiners switch to the host's mode by themselves.
[*][b]Downed and revive, team travel, shared conversations, rejoin with your gear.[/b]
[*][b]Runs on Revision's own code.[/b] RevNet never edits Revision's files; it installs beside them.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Three classes[/b][/size]
Each player picks a class in the launcher.
[list]
[*][b]JC's kind:[/b] the nano-augmented agent, as Deus Ex made him.
[*][b]The Mech:[/b] Gunther and Anna's generation, a mechanically augmented heavy. Armour plating, no fall damage, heavier and louder. Its weapons are built in and can't be lost: an arm cannon with fire modes, arm blades with a cutting mode and a guard mode, and FPV drones flown through a gun-camera view that locks targets, up to a swarm of four drones and a lead, with three flight speeds up to 100 mph. It upgrades through its own trees, bought with salvage torn out of robots.
[*][b]The Light Mech:[/b] Adam Jensen's augmentations from Mankind Divided, fourteen modules bought with Praxis: light, quick and quiet.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Coming: the FK-7 Fly Kit[/b][/size]
The Mech's drones are becoming fly kits: cheap clip-on guidance-and-flight kits that turn the explosives you carry (LAMs, GEP rockets, 20 mm rounds, gas, EMP and scramble grenades) into guided munitions that go off exactly as that explosive would. See the videos for the concept renders and the Corvane Ordnance in-setting promo.

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Windows
[*]Steam
[*]Deus Ex: Game of the Year Edition
[*]Deus Ex: Revision (free on Steam for Deus Ex owners)
[/list]
The release contains no Deus Ex or Revision files: the installer uses your own.

[size=5][b]Credits[/b][/size]
RevNet started from [b]deus-ex-coop[/b] by cranebeaker and contributors (HXFix and HXCoop), which builds on [b]HX[/b] by Hanfling, the original Deus Ex co-op mod. Thank you! The Steam relay launcher and the co-op save format come from there; the co-op game itself is new code on Revision's own classes.
Sounds from CC0 sources (credited in the release). Thanks to the Revision team for Revision.

[size=2]Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Revision team, Square Enix or Eidos. Deus Ex and Deus Ex: Revision belong to their owners.[/size]
```
