# Kit Nests In Game Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The six Corvane nests and the SP-2 Burr in the game as real models, with spotter kits, alarms, launching,
rotating patrols and the nests' own behaviours, playable on a test map in co-op and filmed.

**Architecture:** `tools/nest_game.py` bakes each nest piece (built by `tools/nest_riffs.py`'s functions) into a game
mesh whose skins are baked from Revision's own textures (`tools/nest_skin.py`), added to the RevNetMech art package
the way the FK-7 is (`flykit.game`), its game-derived skins filled at install (`mech_art` template/fill). In
UnrealScript a `CoopNest` decoration holds docked FK-7s (`CoopFPVGuided`) and the game's own `BioCell` meshes as attached
decorations; subclasses add each nest's moving parts and rules. Spotters are `CoopFPVGuided` kits that see like the
game's security cameras and raise `CoopNest.Raise`. One live test (`CoopTestNests`) and one show (`CoopTestNestShow`).

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11 + numpy + PIL (tools), UnrealScript (UE1, HCC via `sh tools/build_net.sh`, art via
`sh build/tmp/rebuild_mech.sh`), Blender 4.5.10 for previews, the live co-op harness (`tools/run_net_live.ps1`,
`tools/run_net_suite.ps1`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-kit-nests-design.md` (read it with this plan).

**Out of this plan (their own plans next):** kits as JC's and NPCs' inventory gear (throw to launch, vendors, drops,
take apart); the level placements (Liberty Island's UNATCO spotters and scripted opening, NSF gas kits, the 3 x 3 x 3
Quiver pack at Area 51); the Army troopers' new look.

## Global Constraints

- A kit never launches or drops its payload: it crashes in and sets it off where it is (`CoopMechAugDrones.Release`).
- Bio cells are the game's own `DeusExItems.BioCell` mesh and skin, unchanged, gauge out.
- Cells: one a kit for Perch, Warden and each Quiver box; three for the Valise's two kits; four a tier on the Argus;
  the Trellis's cell bank two across, as many high as its column (`nest_riffs.frame_power`).
- No hazard stripes on any nest.
- Names: Corvane Ordnance; LP-1 Perch, MR-3 Trellis, SL-4 Warden, FC-5 Valise, VC-6 Quiver, WC-9 Argus, SP-2 Burr, FK-7.
- Kits held level by the tail in a gimbal arm on the box's back wall; the Trellis's go out of the front; a Quiver
  column is 1 to 3 boxes high with one cap and launches one kit at a time, top first; to reach a lower kit, the ones
  above it lift out, hold 40 units above the cap, and go back in, order kept.
- NPC kits fly at the kits' lowest gear (`CoopFPVGuided`'s slowest speed mode); battery 120 s; spotters turn home at
  half battery; a nest relieves a spotter turning home with another (rotating patrols).
- A spotter going red raises the alarm: nests within `AlarmRadius` launch one armed kit (two on Hard and Realistic) at
  the spotted player. Aggressive Defense ignores unarmed kits.
- Payloads: a dice roll, 20 mm HE mostly; LAM 10 % on Easy/Medium, 30 % on Hard/Realistic.
- Shot down, a kit is wrecked; its nest destroyed, its kits go inert, drop, and can be picked up.
- Game-derived art (Revision's textures, the BioCell) is never shipped: the template carries it blank, the fill bakes
  it from the player's own game at install.
- Build `sh tools/build_net.sh`; live runs take the desktop lock (owner revcoop) through `build/tmp/locked_suite.ps1`;
  afterwards restore the install (`python -I build/tmp/restore_061.py`); commit with the project's git identity
  and the trailer lines in every message; only the scrubbed `public` branch is pushed.

## Review Focus

- Co-op: every machine sees the same docked kits, cells, caps, shutters, lids and launches (a client joining after a
  launch too). Test: `CoopTestNests` step `late` (Task 6).
- A nest destroyed mid-launch or while a Quiver's kits hover: they drop inert, never vanish or fly on. Test: step
  `wreck` (Task 3).
- No room to launch (a Quiver under a low ceiling, a Trellis facing a wall): the nest doesn't launch that kit, it
  logs "blocked", nothing goes off at the nest. Test: step `blocked` (Task 3).
- The target dies or leaves with kits in the air: they go home and dock, they don't wander till their battery dies.
  Test: step `lost` (Task 3).
- Nests in a level the players travel away from and back to keep their docked kits (no refill, no duplicates). Test:
  step `travel` (Task 6).
